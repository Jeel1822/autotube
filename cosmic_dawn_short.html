Why Ancient Light Reveals Time

As light travels billions of years across an expanding universe, space itself stretches the light waves toward the red end of the spectrum (Redshift). By capturing infrared photons from galaxies formed just 300 million years after the Big Bang, JWST acts as a cosmic time machine.

Short Production Hook Strategy
