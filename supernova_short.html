Phases of Cosmic Impact

Rigorous scientific vet of the three destructive stages when a massive star detonates in Earth's kill zone.

Time: t = 0 (Instantaneous)

The Flash (Optical & UV)

Blinding light brighter than a billion suns hits Earth instantly, vaporizing the upper ozone layer and exposing the surface to lethal solar and cosmic radiation.

Time: Days to Years

The Cosmic Ray Shower

High-energy particles arrive, destroying remaining ozone, triggering global climate cooling via nitrate aerosols, and causing mass extinction of marine and surface life.

Time: Millennia Aftermath

The Remnant

A glowing neutron star or black hole is left behind in the stellar cradle, while Earth's biosphere takes millennia to recover its complex ecosystems.
